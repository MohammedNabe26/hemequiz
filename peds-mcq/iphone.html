Peds MCQ — iPhone

1. Notifications every 30 min 📅 Add to iPhone Calendar
  1. Tap the button → Subscribe → Add.
  2. Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Subscribed Calendars → Peds MCQ → turn Remove Alerts OFF.
  3. Settings → Notifications → Calendar → Allow.

Every 30 min, 08:00–23:30: “Peds MCQ · Q12”. Tap it to open that question.

2. Install the app Open the app
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Open the app → Share ⬆️ → Add to Home Screen.
  3. First time only: choose the file peds-mcq-data.json (save it to Files first).
